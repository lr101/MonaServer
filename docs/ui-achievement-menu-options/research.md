# Achievement and group-action research

These are adaptations to Stick-It's warm Material/Signika design, not copies of another product's screens.

## First-party references

- [Strava Trophy Case](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15402068-the-strava-trophy-case): a dedicated collection, recent rewards on the profile, tap a badge for details. Informs the badge-grid trial and the separation of completed rewards from ongoing goals.
- [Duolingo achievement redesign](https://blog.duolingo.com/achievement-badges/): milestones of different difficulty and a collection of rewards on the profile. Informs compact category badges and the tier-ladder experiment. Its decorative characters are not appropriate for the user's current professional-icon direction.
- [Google Play Games achievements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3129939?hl=en): accomplishments and XP, recent progress as an entry to the complete achievement collection. Informs the concise progress-list and focused-goal trials. The precise layouts here are our design experiments, not a claim about Google's current screen layout.
- [Signal group management](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360007319331-Group-chats): group descriptions, invitations, admin controls, and membership are distinct tasks. [Group links](https://signal.org/blog/group-links/) provide a specific shareable invitation. Informs relocating each action according to its job rather than moving the original mixed overflow unchanged.

## Five achievement treatments

1. Badge grid: current milestone in a two-column trophy collection; all tiers available through the catalog.
2. Compact goals: Ready to collect, Next up, Collected sections with progress rows and reward previews.
3. Expandable categories: each track opens all its milestones inline.
4. Focused goal: one prominent ready/nearest milestone, quieter list beneath.
5. Tier ladder: horizontally selectable categories with an ordered vertical progression.

## Five group-action treatments

1. Direct icon strip: Share, admin Edit, Report, Leave beside stats. No overflow.
2. Contextual split: Share and admin Edit beside stats; Report and Leave in Group information.
3. Settings destination: Share beside stats; admin Edit, Report, Leave in a separate settings tab.
4. Information hub: no header action strip; Share, admin Edit, Report, Leave inside Group information.
5. Split rows: Share/admin Edit beside stats, Report/Leave on the description footer.

Only the group admin sees Edit. Invitation sharing requires an invite link. Leave retains confirmation and root-route recovery. No action is deleted merely to save space. Achievement details retain requirements, exact progress, reward information, and collection.
