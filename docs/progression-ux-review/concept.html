How do rewards and XP work?

Goals track actions such as mapped pins. Reaching a goal makes its reward ready. Collecting adds XP and a badge; choose separately whether to display the badge on your profile. XP contributes to your level.

Who can change this?

Group members earn styles together. An admin chooses which style appears on the group's map pins. Members can still see the active design.

What this tests

  1. Ready rewards appear first. Future thresholds remain findable without pushing an available action several screens down.
  2. Reward and profile choice are separate. The small badge preview shows what will actually be displayed.
  3. Appearance uses one active state. Selecting a style previews it; Apply changes the group. The normal and gone markers stay in view.

This concept keeps manual reward collection and admin controlled group styles. The report discusses alternatives, including automatic XP and a fuller preset workflow.
